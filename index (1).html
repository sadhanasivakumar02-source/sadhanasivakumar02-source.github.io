Growing Coca-Cola's healthy drinks in Indonesia

  • Marketing strategy
  • Market research
  • TalentGeist x NYC Work Simulation
Problem
How can Coca-Cola grow its healthy beverage portfolio in Indonesia?
Approach
I made the case for growth (functional drinks growing about 8.8% a year, soft drinks declining, a sweetened drinks tax from 2027). I then tested five barriers and kept the two marketing can actually fix: taste perception and competition.
Solution
I scored four marketing strategies against those barriers and chose experiential sampling and creator partnerships. A three-phase, $10M plan reallocates spend using year-1 results.

Two physical AI ideas for Singapore's energy security

  • Physical AI
  • Sustainable design
  • SUTD Design·AI Challenge
Problem
How might Singapore get affordable, sustainable electricity for the long term?
Idea 1: AERIS-01
A solar panel with a rain-harvesting TENG layer that tilts to the best angle for sun or rain. A microcontroller sleeps until the weather changes, and a mechanical latch holds the angle at zero power.
Idea 2: DESIC-0W
An air conditioner add-on with two rotating desiccant beds. One dries the air while the other is regenerated with the AC's waste heat. A weight trigger wakes the AI only when a bed is full.
Decision
I compared both ideas on whether they are Real, Win and Worth, and chose DESIC-0W. Air conditioners use about 30% of household electricity, and TENG yields from rain are still very small.
Limits
DESIC-0W would be hard to retrofit across different aircon models. As first-time builders, we focused on feasibility and listed what we would need to test.

Ideas were human-made. Only the visuals were created with AI assistance.
